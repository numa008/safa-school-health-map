# Methodology

Safa School Health Map uses a school-level vulnerability scoring approach to identify waste-linked climate-health exposure risks for children.

## Core Risk Indicators

Each school is assessed using indicators such as:

1. Proximity to landfill or dumping sites
2. Reported open-burning incidents
3. Visible unmanaged waste nearby
4. Waste collection coverage gaps
5. School waste-management practices
6. Community-reported odor, vectors, or pollution concerns
7. Recyclable waste recovery potential
8. Readiness for practical intervention

## Risk Categories

Schools are classified into four categories:

- Low
- Medium
- High
- Critical

## Scoring Approach

Each indicator receives a score. The combined score generates the school’s overall exposure category. The model is designed to be simple, explainable, and adaptable for local governments, schools, and community organizations.

## Privacy

No child-level personal data is required. Any sensitive school or community data should be anonymized or aggregated before publication.
